## How to use this

Each claim has: the claim, what would **confirm** it, what would **disconfirm** it, and whether **RALF can test it**. The last column is the strategic one — it tells you which claims your own instrument can speak to versus which require borrowed evidence or remain interpretive.

**This ledger is the testable layer.** The wide thesis — musicking as antidote to the fragmenting modes of cognition (abstraction, language, analysis, goal-directedness) — is the motivation, and it lives one layer up (see the thesis page). It is recorded here as **Framing F0** so it is never lost, but it is explicitly *not* something RALF adjudicates. The discipline: of the whole family of fragmenting modes, the ledger tracks the one with a measurable handle — **goal-directedness** — and holds the rest as the argument that makes the measurement worth doing.

## Framing F0 — The wide thesis (named, not tested)

**Claim.** A family of cognitive modes — conceptual abstraction, language, analysis, and goal-directed striving — fragments a person from body, self, and community when it runs unmoored from re-grounding practice, and musicking is an antidote to that fragmentation.

**Confirm / disconfirm.** Argued from literature (e.g. McGilchrist on hemispheric imbalance and the unchecked analytic mode; Carse on finite vs. infinite games; Small on musicking; Ehrenreich on suppressed collective practice). Not resolvable by experiment as stated — too broad, too many construals of "fragmentation."

**RALF can test?** **No, by design.** F0 is the motivating argument, not a result. It earns its place by *generating* testable narrowings (Claim 1 below is the first: it takes the one mode — goal-directedness — that has a measurable physiological signature). Rule: when the urge is to expand F0, convert it into a specific claim with a measurable handle and file it below, or leave it as motivation. Never cite F0 as something RALF showed.

## Claim 1 — Individual desync/resync via sleep

**Claim.** Goal-directed waking activity produces measurable physiological desynchronization in the individual, and sleep restores it. *(This is the testable narrowing of F0: goal-directedness is the member of the fragmenting family with a literal physiological signature.)*

**Confirm.** Established: slow-wave sleep as large-scale neural synchronization; Synaptic Homeostasis Hypothesis (Tononi & Cirelli). EEG phase is a literally measured quantity here.

**Disconfirm.** Evidence that waking does not desynchronize, or that sleep's function is unrelated to re-synchronization.

**RALF can test?** No. Borrowed from neuroscience. This is the *analogy anchor*, not RALF's contribution. It establishes that "desync during effort, resync during repair" is a real pattern somewhere, licensing the analogy.

---

## Claim 2 — Music induces interpersonal synchrony

**Claim.** Shared rhythmic media bring individuals into stable phase relationships with one another (entrainment).

**Confirm.** Neural entrainment to musical rhythm (Large). Synchronized heart rate / breathing in choirs (Vickhoff et al., choir singing and HRV). Interpersonal physiological synchrony literature.

**Disconfirm.** Groups exposed to shared rhythm failing to show above-chance phase-locking; synchrony no greater than coincidence.

**RALF can test?** **YES — this is the load-bearing claim and RALF's first target.** See Artifact 3.

---

## Claim 3 — Synchrony produces prosocial bonding

**Claim.** Interpersonal synchronization increases social cohesion, trust, cooperation.

**Confirm.** McNeill (*Keeping Together in Time*) — synchronized movement builds cohesion. Durkheim's collective effervescence. Turner's communitas. Experimental social-psych on synchrony and cooperation.

**Disconfirm.** See Objection A — synchrony serving destructive ends is the live threat.

**RALF can test?** Partially. RALF can detect *whether* locking occurs; measuring downstream bonding needs additional behavioral measures. Candidate for version N.

---

## Claim 4 — Ritual impoverishment has measurable costs

**Claim.** Cultures impoverished in music/ritual show more disease, conflict, and fragmentation because the repair mechanism is missing.

**Confirm.** Suggestive: Putnam (*Bowling Alone*, social capital decline); Ehrenreich (*Dancing in the Streets*, suppression of collective ecstatic ritual by hierarchical power).

**Disconfirm.** High-ritual societies with high disease/violence; low-ritual societies that are healthy and peaceful. **Confounds are severe** — economic, political, ecological factors all compete. Deaths-of-despair (Case & Deaton) is suggestive at most and cannot be cleanly attributed.

**RALF can test?** No. Most original and least-supported claim. Treat as motivating hypothesis, hold loosely, do not overclaim.

---

## Claim 5 — Afrodiaspora rhythmic practice as high technology

**Claim.** Capoeira, bomba, batá and related traditions are not folklore illustrating the theory; they are sophisticated, centuries-refined indigenous technologies of exactly the synchronization-restoration the thesis describes.

**Confirm.** Ethnomusicological analysis of bomba's *piquetes* (dancer leading drummer = bidirectional coupling made explicit), capoeira's call-and-response phase structure, batá/orisha rhythmic summoning. Demonstrable in RALF by modeling these as coupled-oscillator systems.

**Disconfirm.** Structural analysis showing these practices do *not* exhibit the coupling/entrainment dynamics claimed.

**RALF can test?** Partially, and this is where *your* authority is highest and the field's is lowest. RALF can model and visualize the coupling structure of these forms. Highest-novelty contribution.

# Objections (write these first, answer later)

## Objection A — The Nuremberg problem *(strongest objection — sit with it)*

Synchrony can serve a fascist rally as readily as a healing circle. Martial drumming, military drill, the Nuremberg rallies — synchronization weaponized. **If "harmonization" can produce a Nuremberg rally, then synchrony is not intrinsically prosocial, and the theory needs a second variable explaining *what* is being synchronized toward.** McNeill is honest about this; you must be too. This is not a flaw to hide — it is the most fertile crack in the framework. The theory is incomplete until it names the second variable (candidate directions: open vs. closed coupling? mutual vs. one-way? voluntary vs. coerced? Toward whom is the boundary drawn?). **The wide thesis (F0) supplies a strong candidate: finite vs. infinite game.** The Nuremberg rally is synchrony harnessed to a *finite* game — drilled toward winning, conquest, a settled end; goal-directedness re-imposed at the collective scale. Musicking as RALF means it is synchrony in service of an *infinite* game — continued play, open boundaries. If that distinction holds up, the second variable is not a property of the locking itself but of *what the locking serves*: re-grounding (infinite) versus weaponized goal-directedness (finite). This is where F0 pays rent in the testable layer — it predicts where to look for the missing variable.

## Objection B — Metaphor masquerading as mechanism

The original framing used "frequency / phase / vibration" in both physical and metaphorical registers, letting the shared vocabulary imply a shared mechanism. *Resolved in principle* by grounding RALF in real sampled signals (then frequency really is cycles-per-second of a measured quantity), but stay vigilant: any claim that drifts back to "frequency of the social field" with no measured quantity behind it has relapsed into metaphor-as-mechanism.

## Objection C — Confounds in the cultural claim

Claim 4 cannot be cleanly tested because ritual-poverty co-varies with industrialization, inequality, ecological disruption, etc. Any correlation between ritual impoverishment and disease/conflict has many competing explanations. Hold Claim 4 as hypothesis, not result.

## Objection D — Are you doing scholarship or cosmology?

Recurring self-check. The framework must keep spinning off specific, testable, well-cited claims (this ledger) rather than expanding into a totalizing personal system. When the urge is to add metaphysical scope, add a testable claim instead.
