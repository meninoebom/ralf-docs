## Two layers — read these together

RALF carries a **wide argument** and a **narrow, testable claim**, and they must not be collapsed into each other. The wide argument is the reason the project matters; the narrow claim is the part the instrument can actually speak to. Keeping them distinct is what keeps RALF scholarship rather than a manifesto — *and* keeps it from shrinking into "getting people to move their arms in time."

### The wide argument (the literature layer — motivating, not tested)

> Certain modes of cognition — conceptual abstraction, language, analysis, and goal-directed striving — fragment a person from their body, their core self, and their community when they run unmoored from any practice that re-grounds them. **Musicking is an antidote to that fragmentation.** It is not the only one (sleep, ritual, and contemplative practice are siblings), but it is the one RALF works in. This is the broad thesis about what music is *for*. It is argued from the literature, not adjudicated by the instrument, and it is held loosely and honestly as motivation — never smuggled in as a RALF measurement.

### The operational thesis (the narrow layer — what RALF tests)

> Of that whole family of fragmenting modes, **goal-directedness is the one with a measurable handle.** Goal-directed individual and collective activity produces measurable physiological and social desynchronization, and music/ritual are evolved or invented technologies for restoring synchronization, functionally analogous to sleep. Cultures vary in the availability of these technologies, and this variation predicts variation in stress, health, and conflict.

RALF isolates goal-directedness because it can be operationalized; it does **not** claim that goal-directedness is the *whole* of what fragments us. It is the testable slice of a wider argument the documents keep visible above it.

### Finite and infinite games — why RALF's own activity matters

Goal-directedness is the structure of a **finite game** (Carse): played to win, bounded, settled. The musicking that happens *inside* RALF is a **non-goal-directed, infinite game** — played to continue play. So RALF does two things at once: it treats goal-directedness as the desynchronizing variable it measures, and its own practice *enacts* the non-goal-directed antidote rather than only describing it. The instrument studies the disease and runs the experiment in the cure.

## The one-sentence version

RALF is an instrument for studying goal-directedness as a desynchronizing force and musicking as re-synchronization — treating Afrodiaspora rhythmic practice as a sophisticated technology of social re-grounding. (Interpersonal entrainment is the *measurable mechanism* it instruments, not the whole of what it is about.)

## Working definitions

These are not borrowed metaphors. In RALF the signals are real — body position from motion capture, audio envelopes, sensor streams — so every term below refers to a quantity the system actually samples.

**Oscillation.** A system returning to a similar state at roughly regular intervals. If it cycles, it oscillates. A heartbeat, a neuron's membrane potential, a limb swinging in capoeira.

**Frequency.** Cycles per unit time (Hertz = cycles per second). Always the frequency *of a specific measured quantity*. In RALF, never a frequency of nothing — always of a signal being sampled (joint angle over time, an audio envelope, a body's vertical displacement).

**Phase.** Where in its cycle an oscillation currently is, expressed as an angle (0–360° or 0–2π). Two oscillators at the *same frequency* can sit at different points in their cycle. The phase *difference* between them is the whole story of synchrony. Phase is not "how fast" — it is "where in the cycle, relative to another cycle."

**Phase-locking / synchronization.** When two oscillators hold a *constant phase relationship* over time. They need not be at the same phase — they need a *steady* difference. Two dancers in perfect anti-phase are perfectly synchronized because the relationship is stable. Synchronization means **the relationship is locked**, not that the states are identical. Capoeira players are rarely doing the same movement; they hold a phase relationship in call-and-response. That is synchronization in the precise sense.

**Entrainment.** When one oscillator *adjusts* its frequency or phase to match another, and they lock. You move at the drum's 120 BPM and stay there — you have entrained. **This is the *measurable* mechanism of "restoring synchronization":** music provides a shared external oscillator that individuals entrain to, pulling their independent rhythms into a stable phase relationship with each other. That sentence states one mechanism of the thesis in correct technical language — the one RALF can instrument. It is the operational handle on re-synchronization, not the entirety of it. Re-grounding through musicking surely involves more than phase-locking of limbs (attention, affect, meaning, breath); entrainment is simply the part that yields to measurement. Do not let the precision of this one mechanism make it the whole story.

**Coupling.** Two oscillators are coupled if each can influence the other. Social synchrony is *bidirectional* coupling — you adjust to me, I adjust to you, and we settle into a shared rhythm neither of us fully dictated. The difference between everyone-follows-the-drummer (one-way) and a jam where the rhythm emerges between players (mutual coupling). The richest claims live in mutual coupling.

## Mathematical backbone

The formal grounding is **coupled oscillator dynamics**, specifically the **Kuramoto model** (how a population of oscillators spontaneously synchronizes) plus the **neural resonance / entrainment** literature (Large). This is the rigorous version of "many individuals falling into collective rhythm." It makes the project legible to scientists without requiring the contemplative frame to be accepted.

## What this reframes

The intellectual position is not published and then defended while waiting to be recognized. It is **pulled into existence by the artifact.** RALF is not an art project with a manifesto attached; it is a research apparatus that produces measurements about a stated hypothesis. A tool that detects and induces embodied social synchrony is the kind of thing a design-research program can build a position or a PhD around.

## Scope discipline

Two layers are deliberately excluded from the research and kept as private generative ground:

- **The metaphysics** (reality as fields of awareness; Indic/Sufi cosmology). Personal belief system. Not testable, not claimed as science. The engine, not the contribution.
- **The physics analogy** (reality as fundamentally vibration). Explicit analogy only. Nothing load-bearing rests on it.

The failure mode to avoid: wanting the authority of scholarship while doing cosmology — claiming scientific grounding for what is actually contemplative conviction. The cosmology stays sacred; the research stays rigorous; neither corrupts the other.

A *third* layer is not excluded but **demarcated**: the wide argument up top (musicking as antidote to fragmenting modes of cognition). It is public, literature-grounded, and worth arguing — but it is motivation, not a RALF result. It belongs in the prospectus and the writing; it never appears in the claims ledger as something the instrument tested. The discipline is the same in both directions: don't let the cosmology pose as science, and don't let the wide argument pose as a measurement.
